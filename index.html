01 MSc thesis · Open source · 2026

A research platform where the rules stay put.

For my thesis, I needed an RL environment whose behavior I could reproduce, inspect, and compare. I built the simulator underneath it too: a Rust engine with Python bindings, batched stepping, legality masks, explicit observation and action contracts, plus replay tooling for when experiments drift.

03 / About

The technology changes.
The standard doesn’t.

I started in web development and kept moving toward the questions underneath it: What happens when the data changes? When assumptions drift? When software meets people who do not care how clever the architecture is?

That is why my work moves between research infrastructure and client software. The surface changes, but I keep coming back to stable interfaces, explicit trade-offs, validation, testing, and a handover the next person can actually use.
